How to read a play

EV % = how good the price is vs. what the bet is really worth. Higher EV = better deal. Win % = the model's chance this exact bet cashes ("will it win?"). EV tells you the value; Win % tells you the likelihood.

Confidence tier = overall grade (Win % + value):
  • Double Diamond 💎💎 Win > 70% and strong value. Our best plays — but 70% still loses about 1 in every 3–4 bets, so it's not a lock.
  • Diamond 💎 Win 60%+ with solid value. Strong read.
  • Gold Win 55%+. A real edge.
  • Silver Win under 55%. Slight lean — the value is mostly in the price.
